Corresponding sources for the desktop components in Kubemap 0.1.0

The exact installed versions and original metadata are recorded in INDEX.txt
and the python/ subdirectories of the third-party notice directory.

PySide6 6.12.0 and Shiboken6 6.12.0 (unmodified upstream binaries):
https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/pyside6/PySide6-6.12.0-src/pyside-setup-everywhere-src-6.12.0.tar.xz

Qt 6.12.0, including Qt WebEngine and its Chromium third-party sources:
https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/6.12/6.12.0/single/qt-everywhere-src-6.12.0.tar.xz

Kubemap application source, build scripts and lockfiles:
Publish kubemap_0.1.0_source.tar.gz next to kubemap_0.1.0_<architecture>.deb.

Qt/PySide6 shared libraries are unmodified. Replaceable libraries are kept
separate in the PyInstaller onedir installation under /opt/kubemap/.
Preserve all upstream notices when copying or redistributing these files.
When changing dependency versions, update these source references to match.
